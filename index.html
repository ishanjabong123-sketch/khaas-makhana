Khaas Makhana - Premium Fox Nut Exporters from Bihar

Khaas Makhana supplies premium makhana, also known as fox nuts or lotus seeds, from Purnia, Bihar to wholesale buyers, importers, food brands, distributors, and private label businesses. Our work starts in one of India's most important makhana-growing regions and focuses on careful sorting, consistent sizing, clean handling, and reliable bulk supply for domestic and international markets.

Buyers choose our makhana for its natural creamy white color, full round puff, long shelf life, and suitability for roasted snacks, flavored makhana, retail pouches, dry fruit assortments, health food ranges, and food processing applications. We support bulk quote requests, export documentation, custom packaging discussions, and grade selection based on buyer requirements.

Our product range includes export premium 6+ or 7 suta handpicked makhana above 21 mm, retail premium 6 suta makhana from 18 to 21 mm, and commercial makhana grades from 15 to 18 mm. Packaging options include bulk PP bags and vacuum or nitrogen-flushed packets for retail and private label projects.

Premium Makhana Products

Each batch is selected for size, appearance, low breakage, and practical shelf stability. Standard specifications include maximum moisture of 5 to 8 percent, minimum order quantity of 1 ton, shelf life up to 12 months in cool and airtight storage, and broken seeds up to 2 percent depending on grade and order details.

  • Export Premium: 6+ or 7 suta handpicked makhana, above 21 mm.
  • Retail Premium: 6 suta normal and handpicked makhana, 18-21 mm.
  • Commercial: 3/3+, 4/4+, and 5/5+ suta makhana, 15-18 mm.

From Purnia, Bihar to Global Buyers
